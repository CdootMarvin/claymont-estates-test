Life at Claymont

Room to breathe, close to everything

Large, forested lots keep neighbors from feeling stacked on top of each other, while mature trees and green space bring deer, turkey, and other wildlife right through the neighborhood. It's a setting that has aged well since the community was first established, staying understated, well kept, and genuinely quiet.

Our Community

A neighborhood shaped by its setting

Claymont Estates was built in the late 1960s and early 1970s, and is home to approximately 150 households set among wooded lots and green space throughout the neighborhood.
