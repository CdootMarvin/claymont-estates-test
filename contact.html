Subdivision Trustees

Claymont Estates trustees are listed on the City of Chesterfield's official subdivision trustees directory, not on this site. Here's how to find them:

  1. Open the City of Chesterfield's Subdivision Trustees page →
  2. Click on the trustees PDF list on that page.
  3. Search the PDF for "Claymont Estates" (Ctrl+F on a computer, or your browser's find/search on mobile) to find our current trustees and their contact info.
